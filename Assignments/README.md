# IFEXA Data Analysis Academy — Excel Assignment 01 📊

## Assignment Overview
This assignment was given by IFEXA Data Analysis Academy, Cohort 2, to practise Excel mathematical functions, conditional calculations, and basic business analysis using a sales dataset.

## Part A: Mathematical Functions

**Functions Practised:**
- Multiplication
- SUM
- AVERAGE
- MAX
- MIN
- ROUND

**Tasks Completed:**
1. Calculated revenue for each salesperson using Units Sold × Price.
2. Calculated profit using Revenue − Expenses.
3. Calculated total units sold.
4. Calculated total revenue.
5. Calculated total expenses.
6. Calculated average revenue.
7. Identified the highest revenue.
8. Identified the lowest revenue.
9. Rounded the first salesperson's revenue to the nearest thousand naira.

## Part B: SUMIF

**Function Practised:** SUMIF

**Tasks Completed:**
- Calculated total revenue generated in Onitsha.
- Calculated total revenue generated in Awka.
- Calculated total revenue from Laptop sales.
- Calculated total profit from Phone sales.

## Part C: SUMIFS

**Function Practised:** SUMIFS

**Tasks Completed:**
- Calculated total revenue from Laptop sales in Onitsha.
- Calculated total revenue from Phone sales in Awka.
- Calculated total profit from Tablet sales in Onitsha.

## Part D: Business Analysis

**Business Questions Explored:**
- Identified the product that generated the highest total revenue.
- Identified the city that generated the highest total revenue.
- Explained the difference between revenue and profit.
- Explained why businesses need to track expenses.

## Skills Developed
- Applying mathematical functions in Excel.
- Calculating revenue and profit.
- Performing conditional calculations with SUMIF and SUMIFS.
- Using statistical functions to analyse sales data.
- Interpreting sales performance.
- Understanding basic business financial concepts.
